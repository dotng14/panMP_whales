What this is for

We are testing several ways of turning the recording into numbers for a song model. For each query, every method proposes the stretch it finds most similar, at least 5 minutes away. Your judgements tell us how often each method's match really is the same phrase type — a score with a known ceiling of 1, which the automatic tests lack. The page never shows which method proposed a pair, and the order is shuffled.
